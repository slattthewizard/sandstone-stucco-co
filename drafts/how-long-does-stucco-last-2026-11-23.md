# How Long Does Stucco Last? What Calgary Firms Say

Ask three contractors how long stucco lasts and you may get three different numbers. The published figures we found run from about 25 years in a national guide to 50 years from a Calgary firm, and nobody can tell you which one applies to your wall without looking at it. This page sets out what the firms actually say, why they disagree, and what the evidence in your own wall can tell you.

So the published figures for walls differ by a factor of two. That's a wide range, and there are good reasons for it.

## Why the claims conflict

### They describe different things

A firm quoting "natural lifespan" may mean the coating if nothing goes wrong. Another may mean the time before a typical owner starts spending real money on repairs. Neither is wrong, but they aren't the same measure.

### Nobody is publishing test data

None of the firms above points to independent testing for its figure. Under the Act's rule on lifespan representations, that is exactly why they should be read as a firm's view rather than a measured result.

### Systems differ

Calgary Parging said in 2024 that cement stucco with an acrylic finish is the main product on over 87% of the residential properties it works on, going by its own jobs. That isn't a city survey. Martini Stone Masonry names the systems it meets as "conventional three-coat, IMASCO or EIFS." Three-coat, acrylic-finished and EIFS walls are built differently, and a single number is unlikely to fit all of them.

### Conditions differ

Calgary has a harsh climate for cement products. The City recorded 117 freeze-thaw cycles in 2022, against a 30-year average of 128, and 54 chinook days against an average of 46. A wall that gets rain, snow and sun on every side ages differently from a sheltered one.

### Workmanship and maintenance differ

The same product can last much longer on a wall with good flashing and drainage than on one without. That is hard to put in a published number.

## What shortens a stucco wall's life

The failure modes below are all contractors' claims, repeated here with attribution. They are useful because they point at things you can check.

### Water getting behind the stucco

Calgary Parging says: "Stucco is designed to deflect water, not to be waterproof. The layer that actually keeps water out of your home is the building paper behind it." The firm also says over 30% of its residential repairs address water damage caused by incorrectly installed rain gutters, eaves or flashing, based on its own 2024 jobs. Calgary Stucco and Slate says nobody can tell you the full extent of water damage until the wall is opened up. Our [stucco repair](/stucco-repair-calgary/) page explains what that looks like in practice.

### Freeze-thaw cracking

Martini says water penetrates small cracks, freezes at -20°C and expands. The City of Calgary says stucco does not handle moisture well, and that moisture and snow can cause it to shrink and crack. A small crack that is left open lets in more water each winter.

### Snow against the base

Martini says snow piled against stucco softens and crumbles the lowest 12 to 18 inches of the wall.

### Gaps at windows and doors

Martini says temperature movement opens 3 to 8 mm gaps where stucco meets frames. Those joints need sealing or flashing that holds.

### Impact damage

Martini says golf-ball-sized hail, most common in July and August, leaves 2 to 5 mm indentations that break the moisture barrier. The City of Calgary says stucco will hold up well to flying debris in moderate wind and hail, but severe storms can cause damage, and that all communities in Calgary are equally exposed to hail. The Insurance Bureau of Canada estimated insured losses from the 5 August 2024 Calgary hailstorm at $3.25 billion in February 2025, and the industry loss was later put at CAD 3.349 billion by CatIQ. Our article on [hail damage to stucco](/blog/hail-damage-stucco-calgary/) goes through what to look for.

### Birds

Calgary Parging says flickers work Calgary stucco hard, especially on west and south elevations in spring. Flickers are federally protected, so don't harm them or disturb a nest with eggs or chicks. Our [woodpecker damage article](/blog/woodpecker-damage-stucco-calgary/) covers what is allowed.

### High-pressure washing

Weststar Wall Systems says high-pressure water can damage stucco and force moisture inside.

Some cracking is not a failure at all. Imasco, a cement stucco manufacturer, says some cracking and efflorescence are inherent to portland cement and are not a reflection of product quality. A fine hairline crack is a thing to watch, not necessarily a sign the wall is at the end of its life.

## Signs worth checking on your own wall

Walk the house on a dry day and look at each elevation.

- Soft or spongy areas, and peeling or bubbling. Weststar lists these as warning signs.
- Cracks wider than 1/4 inch, or stucco crumbling off the wall. High End Construction, a Calgary firm, says these are signs of deeper issues.
- Staining below windows, under eaves and at the base of walls.
- Hollow sounds when you tap the surface lightly.
- Gaps around window and door frames.
- Anything that has changed since last year. Photograph it with a tape measure beside it, and take the same photo again later.

None of these tells you how many years are left. They tell you where to ask a contractor to look.

## Repair or replace: the tests Calgary firms use

Total Exteriors offers a straightforward way to think about it. It suggests repair when:

- the damage is localized
- moisture intrusion is minimal and hasn't spread
- the majority of your stucco is structurally sound

It suggests replacement when damage is widespread or structural, when moisture problems are extensive, or when the stucco is beyond its natural lifespan, which the firm puts at 40 to 50 years. Notice that the last test leans on the lifespan claim itself. That's one more reason to ask any contractor who says a wall is "too old" what they actually found.

This site can't tell you whether your wall needs replacing, and no general statement can. A contractor looking at the wall, the paper and the framing behind it can tell you more, and you can ask them to put the reasons in writing.

Say a south wall has several patched cracks, a bulge near a window and soft spots at the base. Localized repairs could still be an option if the rest of the wall is sound, and replacement could be the option if the moisture is widespread. A contractor may price both, and the two prices tell you what the choice is worth. Or say a house has had a single section replaced after hail and the rest of the stucco is intact. That is a repair, not a lifespan question. These are hypothetical examples, not customer stories.

## Extending the life of a wall you already have

Little of this is complicated. Keep gutters and downspouts working and pointed away from the wall. Keep snow off the base. Seal and monitor cracks and window joints, and don't pressure wash. Book repairs for the season when contractors can do them properly: Imasco says no stucco work below 4°C for 24 hours before, during and after application, and Calgary Parging says the season is typically April through October.

If hail or another insured event is involved, the City of Calgary advises contacting your home insurance provider before you do any repair work. This site does not handle, file or negotiate insurance claims. If your home is newer, check your new home warranty before paying for repairs, since whether a problem is a defect is for the warranty provider.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does stucco last in Calgary?

There is no tested figure. Total Exteriors, a Calgary firm, says 40 to 50 years, HomeStars' national siding guide says acrylic stucco typically lasts about 25 years, and Calgary Parging says properly applied parging lasts 15 to 25 years. These are the firms' own claims, and the condition of your wall, its drainage and the system it uses matter more than any number.

### Why do contractors give different stucco lifespans?

They may be measuring different things, such as the coating's natural lifespan or the time before repairs start, and they work with different systems. None of the firms cites independent testing for its figure. A written inspection of your own wall is more useful than a general number.

### Is old stucco always a reason to replace it?

Not by itself. Total Exteriors says to repair when damage is localized, moisture is minimal and most of the stucco is structurally sound. It suggests replacement when damage is widespread, moisture is extensive or the stucco is past its natural lifespan.

### Can regular repairs make stucco last longer?

Calgary firms say water getting behind the wall is a main cause of damage, so sealing cracks, keeping gutters working and fixing flashing are the usual priorities. We can't promise a result, and a contractor should explain in writing what a repair will and won't do.
